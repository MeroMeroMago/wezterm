## 2. Clicks keyboard cases & Clicks Power Keyboard (Bluetooth) — pairing, compatibility, and which phones suit the user's existing Clicks Bluetooth keyboard

### Takeaway
There are two product lines. **Keyboard cases** are model-specific and connect through the phone's port or case. They exist for iPhone 14–17, Pixel 9/9 Pro, Motorola Razr and Galaxy S25, at $99–$159. The **Power Keyboard** is universal: a Bluetooth keyboard that attaches magnetically via MagSafe/Qi2 and pairs with up to 3 devices. It works with any Bluetooth phone, but it fits and feels best on a **MagSafe/Qi2 slab phone** that has no keyboard of its own. It makes little sense on a Titan or Communicator, which already have keyboards.

### Cited Findings
- **Power Keyboard compatibility:** works with "any Bluetooth-enabled device": iPhones, iPads, Android phones and tablets, smart TVs and AR/VR headsets. Clicks tested it with iPhone Air, 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max, 16 Pro, "all other MagSafe compatible iPhones", Pixel 9, Galaxy Fold and others. — [Clicks Power Keyboard page](https://www.clicks.tech/powerkeyboard); [Clicks product page](https://clicks.tech/products/powerkeyboard)
- Notebookcheck headline: "Clicks Power Keyboard turns any phone with **MagSafe or Qi2** into a BlackBerry." — [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Clicks-Power-Keyboard-turns-any-phone-with-MagSafe-or-Qi2-into-a-BlackBerry.1195224.0.html)
- **Pairing:** hold the "123" key, then press "0" to enter pairing mode. It connects to **up to 3 devices** and switches between them with a keyboard shortcut. — [Cybernews Power Keyboard review](https://cybernews.com/reviews/clicks-power-keyboard-review/); [CNN Underscored](https://www.cnn.com/cnn-underscored/reviews/clicks-power-keyboard)
- **Known quirk:** one reviewer switching between Android and iPhone found it wouldn't pair to the iPhone until they pressed a number key plus the power button to add a second Bluetooth slot. — [CNN Underscored](https://www.cnn.com/cnn-underscored/reviews/clicks-power-keyboard)
- TechCrunch (Aug 10, 2026): "brings BlackBerry-style typing to any phone — **with some compromises**." — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/10/clicks-power-keyboard-brings-blackberry-style-typing-to-any-phone-with-some-compromises/)
- **Price:** pre-order was $79, rising to $109 when shipping began in spring 2026. — [Cybernews keyboard case review](https://cybernews.com/reviews/clicks-keyboard-case-review/). One August 2026 review headline says "$99". — [AndroGuider](https://www.androguider.com/2026/08/clicks-power-keyboard-review-blackberry.html). **Conflict:** $99 vs $109. The retail price may have changed or the review may be citing a sale.
- **Keyboard cases:** available for iPhone 17, 16, 15 and 14, Google Pixel, Motorola Razr, and Samsung Galaxy (S25). Prices: iPhone $139 (small) / $159 (Pro Max); Pixel 9/9 Pro and Razr 2024/2025 $139; Galaxy S25 and Razr+ launched at $99, rising to $139. — [Cybernews keyboard case review](https://cybernews.com/reviews/clicks-keyboard-case-review/); [TechCrunch, Feb 2025 (Android launch)](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/25/clicks-brings-its-blackberry-style-keyboard-case-to-android-users); [Amazon listing, iPhone 15/16e](https://www.amazon.com/Clicks-Keyboard-iPhone-15-Experience/dp/B0DPLXYVX3)
- The Sept 18, 2026 Communicator update also announced an "expanded ecosystem" and new accessories. — [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Clicks-Communicator-gets-upgraded-specs-and-new-accessories-ahead-of-December-release.1402870.0.html)

### Inferences
- The user already owns a Clicks Bluetooth keyboard, most likely the Power Keyboard. The best host phones are those with **built-in MagSafe/Qi2 magnets** and no keyboard of their own: iPhone 12 and later, and recent Qi2-magnet Android phones such as the Pixel 10 series (**[training data, unverified]**). The keyboard holds on magnetically, so no specific case is needed.
- Pairing it with a **small, cheap, minimal Android phone** (for example, a stripped-down Pixel with a minimal launcher) gives most of the Communicator's typing benefit today, without waiting until December or paying $649. Because it's detachable, the phone can also be used as a plain slab.
- Pairing it with the Titan 2 / Titan 2 Elite, Minimal Phone or Communicator would be redundant, since those have built-in keyboards. It would only help as a desk or tablet keyboard.
- For screen-time reduction, a detachable keyboard adds typing speed but no friction. It won't limit usage by itself.

### Gaps
- Couldn't verify the Power Keyboard's battery or pass-through charging, weight, or behavior on non-magnetic phones (a MagSafe ring would presumably be needed).
- No confirmed list of which Android phones have built-in Qi2 magnets as of Sept 2026.
- Whether the Power Keyboard gets any special integration with the Communicator (e.g., shared shortcuts) is unknown.
