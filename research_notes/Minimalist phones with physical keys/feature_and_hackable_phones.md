## 1. Best current US-compatible keypad feature phones and which essentials they support

### Takeaway
No true T9/flip dumbphone in the US does everything: the "managed Android flip" category (Sunbeam F1, Kyocera DuraXV Extreme+, Cat S22 Flip, Qin F21/F22 Pro) can do hotspot, group MMS and some navigation (Sunbeam = Waze), and the open ones (Qin, Kyocera, Cat) can sideload/run WhatsApp — but **none support Google Wallet/Apple Pay tap-to-pay**, and banking/rideshare/2FA apps are "maybe, via sideload, with a painful UI" at best. Plain KaiOS/Verizon flips (Nokia/HMD 2780, TCL Flip 2/3, Kyocera Cadence) keep calls/SMS/MMS/hotspot but have lost WhatsApp.

## 3. Hackable/DIY phones with physical keys — can any be a daily phone, and what do they lose?

### Takeaway
The BlackBerry-keyboard Pi handhelds (**HackberryPi CM5**, **Beepy**, ClockworkPi) are Linux pocket computers, **not phones** — HackberryPi and Beepy have no cellular radio at all, and the uConsole's 4G add-on can place calls only via raw AT commands and a headset. The only candidates that can be real daily phones are the **Zinwa Q25** (Android on BlackBerry Classic hardware), Android keyboard phones, and Linux phones (PinePhone/Librem 5) — the latter with flaky VoLTE and no mainstream apps.

### Cited Findings

## 4. De-Googled Android (GrapheneOS, /e/OS, LineageOS): tap-to-pay and banking

### Takeaway
Google Wallet tap-to-pay does **not** work on GrapheneOS (Play Integrity/attestation fails for non-Google-certified OS); most major US banking apps reportedly do work via sandboxed Google Play. US tap-to-pay alternatives are thin: Curve Pay is Europe/UK-focused; practical US workarounds are a smartwatch (Garmin Pay; possibly a Wear OS watch) or a physical card.

### Cited Findings
- "Tap-to-pay through Google Wallet needs an attestation a custom OS can't produce"; workarounds = smartwatch with its own payment app, bank app with its own NFC path, or physical card — [franklinetech.com compatibility guide](https://franklinetech.com/what-breaks-on-grapheneos/) (secondary)
- GrapheneOS ships without Play Services; sandboxed Google Play is optional/user-installed — [factually.co](https://factually.co/product-reviews/electronics-tech/use-google-pay-on-grapheneos-0d2e9b) (low-quality aggregator); official doc [grapheneos.org/usage](https://grapheneos.org/usage) blocked
- Claimed working US banks in 2026 via sandboxed Play: Chase, Amex, Discover, Wells Fargo, Navy Federal — [vucense.com GrapheneOS Banking Apps 2026](https://vucense.com/tech-reviews/mobile-gear/grapheneos-banking-apps-2026-complete-compatibility-list/) (snippet only; not fetched)
- GrapheneOS forum thread on Wallet/Google Pay — [discuss.grapheneos.org](https://discuss.grapheneos.org/d/475-wallet-google-pay)
- Contactless via Curve Pay, PayPal and bank apps on GrapheneOS (UK author, June 2025) — [Terence Eden, shkspr.mobi](https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2025/06/contactless-payments-with-grapheneos/); HN: "Contactless payments already work on GrapheneOS via Curve Pay, PayPal and the ap[p]…" — [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47216661)
