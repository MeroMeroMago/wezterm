## 1. Light Phone III: price, shipping, NFC/tap-to-pay, tools, messaging, carriers, reviews

### Takeaway
The Light Phone III has been shipping since March 2025 and is still sold with a backlog. Recent listings show **$899 unlocked**, or from $59/mo on a 2-year Light plan (older sources say $699 pre-order and $799 retail). It has an **NFC chip, but tap-to-pay is still NOT enabled** as of Sept 2026: it is "future" functionality with no announced date. It covers the most "modern essentials" of any non-Android minimalist phone: Directions, Rideshare (Uber/Lyft), Authenticator (2FA), Hotspot, Music, Podcasts, Notes, and a camera. It has **no WhatsApp, Signal, iMessage, browser, or banking apps**.

### Inferences

- The LP3 covers maps, rideshare, 2FA, hotspot, and group SMS/MMS. It cannot cover banking apps, WhatsApp, Signal, or iMessage. For iPhone-heavy social circles, group-chat friction is the most-cited real-world pain point.
- The 2FA Authenticator helps with TOTP codes. It does not help with bank-app push approvals or app-bound 2FA such as Duo push or bank-app approval flows (inference).

### Gaps

- There is no official statement with a timeline for NFC payments. I could not confirm whether any 2026 LightOS release touched NFC.
- Signal: no native support was found. I found no evidence of a third-party Signal tool.

## 2. Mudita Kompakt (E-ink, MuditaOS K, de-Googled AOSP): sideloading, NFC, reviews

### Takeaway
A 4.3" E-ink, de-Googled AOSP phone that sells for roughly **$439–$549** depending on source and date. It is **shipping, with active 2026 updates** (OS K 1.5 in May, 1.6 in Aug 2026). **You can sideload APKs over USB from a computer** (WhatsApp, Signal, banking, 2FA, Uber). Without Google Play Services, however, many banking and rideshare apps may be flaky or refuse to run. It has NFC hardware, but **there is no Google Wallet and no working tap-to-pay**.

### Cited Findings
- Specs: 4.3" 800x480 E Ink touchscreen, quad-core MediaTek, 3GB RAM / 32GB storage, **8MP camera with dual LED flash**, 3.5mm jack, IP54, 3,300 mAh battery (~6-day standby), fingerprint reader, wireless charging — [New Atlas](https://newatlas.com/mobile-technology/compact-e-ink-phone-mudita-kompakt/); [Liliputing](https://liliputing.com/mudita-kompakt-is-a-minimalist-phone-with-an-e-ink-display-and-modern-features-like-wireless-charging-and-a-fingerprint-reader-crowdfunding/)
- Privacy switch cuts cellular, mics, camera, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth. No browser and no app store — [Pocket-lint](https://www.pocket-lint.com/mudita-kompakt-review/)
- Price: launch retail US$473 (€439) — [New Atlas](https://newatlas.com/mobile-technology/compact-e-ink-phone-mudita-kompakt/); "$439" in a Feb 2026 review — [What If I Didn't](https://whatifididnt.com/blog/mudita-kompakt/); $549 in an aggregated 2026 roundup — [The Gadgeteer via search](https://the-gadgeteer.com/2026/05/24/best-e-ink-phones-worth-buying-may-2026/); ~$384 at a third-party EU retailer — [Techinn](https://www.tradeinn.com/techinn/en/mudita-kompakt-mobile-phone/143106133/p). **Price varies. The writer should present it as a ~$440–$550 range.** Also sold on [Amazon US](https://www.amazon.com/clp/B0FFN6N5KP) and [dumbwireless](https://dumbwireless.com/products/mudita-kompakt).
- Sideloading: MuditaOS K has no on-device APK installer. You sideload from a computer over USB-C, which enables apps "like WhatsApp, banking apps, or 2FA tools". Sideloading doesn't harm the device, but issues from it aren't covered by warranty — [Mudita Support: Third-party apps](https://support.mudita.com/en/support/solutions/articles/77000577356-does-mudita-kompakt-support-third-party-apps-); [Mudita Forum sideload instructions](https://forum.mudita.com/t/instructions-on-how-to-sideload-apps-on-mudita-kompakt/8244)

Heavy sideloading "unravels" the device's philosophy — [What If I Didn't](https://whatifididnt.com/blog/mudita-kompakt/); [Dapper & Groomed 3-month review](https://www.dapperandgroomed.com/blog/mudita-kompakt-review-after-3-months-my-honest-take-on-this-minimalist-phone); [PauseGadget](https://pausegadget.com/mudita-kompakt-review/)

Compatible MVNOs include Mint, Noble Mobile, and dumbwireless — [Mudita Support: Global or North American?](https://support.mudita.com/en/support/solutions/articles/77000577355-mudita-kompakt-global-or-north-american-); [Kickstarter FAQ](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/mudita/mudita-kompakt/faqs)

### Inferences
- Banking apps that depend on Google Play Integrity or Firebase push will likely fail or lose notifications on a de-Googled device. Signal and WhatsApp generally run without GMS but may lose instant push (inference; not documented in sources).
- On an 800x480 E-ink screen, sideloaded maps, rideshare, and banking apps will be usable but slow. It's best treated as "possible in a pinch".

### Gaps
- Hotspot support is not confirmed either way in any source I found.
- No Verge, Wired, or Engadget review of the Kompakt surfaced in search.
- I found no per-bank compatibility list. The forum thread exists but its content wasn't readable.
- Group MMS reliability on US carriers is not documented.

## 3. Android E-ink phones with Google Play: Hisense A9/A9 Pro, Bigme HiBreak family, Boox Palma

### Takeaway
These are full Android phones with Google Play, which means WhatsApp, Signal, banking, Uber, maps, and 2FA all work. They are Chinese-market or import devices, **mainly compatible with T-Mobile and its MVNOs** in the US. The **Bigme HiBreak Pro** is the best-documented E-ink phone with **working Google Wallet tap-to-pay** (since firmware v1.2.6). **Boox Palma 2 Pro and Palma 3 are data-only, with no voice calls or SMS and no NFC**, so they are not phones.

## 4. Other "calm" / minimal phones: Minimal Phone, Punkt MC03, Wisephone II, Unplugged, HMD, Daylight, and others

### Takeaway
- **Minimal Phone (Minimal Company)** runs full Android with Google services, so **Google Wallet works**. The Minimal Phone 2 ships in December 2026.
- **Punkt MC03** is de-Googled AphyOS. It has no Google Pay, and a Fidesmo contactless-payment integration is in progress. Its North American launch was targeted for spring/summer 2026.
- **Wisephone II** offers curated apps, including some banking, via a $14.99/mo subscription. Google Wallet is undocumented.
- **Unplugged UP Phone** is a privacy phone, not a minimalist one.
- **HMD Barbie** is a KaiOS flip with no modern apps.
- **Daylight DC-1** is a tablet, not a phone.
- No "Ghost" or "Balance" phone could be verified.

## 5. Which can run Google Wallet (Play Integrity / certification)?

### Takeaway
Google Wallet tap-to-pay requires a Play-certified device passing Play Integrity with GMS.
- **Confirmed working:** Bigme HiBreak Pro (firmware ≥1.2.6, unrooted) and Minimal Phone.
- **Not possible:** Light Phone III (NFC unused, no Android apps), Mudita Kompakt (no GMS), Punkt MC03 (no GMS; Fidesmo alternative pending), Boox Palma 2 Pro/3 (no NFC and not phones), HMD Barbie (KaiOS).
- **Unknown/unconfirmed:** Hisense A9 Pro, HiBreak Plus/S, Wisephone II.

### Cited Findings
- HiBreak Pro Wallet works after v1.2.6. Early units lacked "security clearance" — [Parka Blogs](https://www.parkablogs.com/content/review-bigme-hibreak-pro-e-ink-phone)
- Rooting breaks Wallet on Bigme — [XDA](https://xdaforums.com/t/bigme-hibreak-pro-color-color-s-root-magisk-debloat-general-impressions.4764527/)
- Google Wallet errors flagging devices as "rooted" or "uncertified" stem from Play Integrity (replaced SafetyNet) — [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/error-google-wallet-3213122/); [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2022/09/26/google-wallet-security-requirements-error/)
- Kompakt: no Google services, so no Google Wallet — [Mudita Forum](https://forum.mudita.com/t/nfc-usage-on-the-kompakt/7989)
- MC03: no Google Pay. Fidesmo contactless is in the works — [Punkt FAQ](https://support.punkt.ch/en-US/articles/aphyos-apps-422562)
- Palma 2 Pro: no NFC — [Parka Blogs](https://www.parkablogs.com/content/boox-palma-2-pro-e-reader-review)
- Minimal Phone: Google Wallet works — [adessephone.com](https://adessephone.com/articles/light-phone-alternative/)

### Inferences
- Chinese-market E-ink phones can lose Wallet certification after firmware changes or regional-variant swaps. Buyers should confirm the global/Play-certified ROM before purchase (inference from the Bigme history).

### Gaps
- No Reddit threads on Wallet for Hisense or Bigme could be read (fetch was blocked).
